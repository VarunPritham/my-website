Hello, world — why I started this site

What this site is about, and what you'll find here.

For a long time, my thinking lived in scattered places: notes on my phone, half-formed ideas, long conversations with AI, books I meant to write about. None of it added up to anything I could look back on.

This site is my attempt to change that. It's one place, owned by me, where my ideas, writing and favorite stories connect.

What you'll find here

Ideas: things I want to build. Right now there are four: a personal trading analysis platform to put my CFA learning into practice, a digital self-reflection app to see where my attention really goes, a private recommendation engine that learns from people with similar tastes without exposing anyone's data, and a self-writing work journal that tells me each morning where I left off. They're early, and I'll share progress as they take shape.

Writing: thinking out loud about those ideas, markets, technology and whatever I'm learning.

Shelf: books, films and shows that stayed with me.

Resume: the formal version, for anyone who wants it.

Life is limited, and so is attention. I'd rather spend mine deliberately.

Why write in public

Writing forces clarity. An idea that feels complete in my head often falls apart the moment I try to explain it, and that's exactly when it starts getting better. Sharing early also means feedback arrives while it can still change something.

Some posts will be polished; most won't be. Thanks for stopping by.
