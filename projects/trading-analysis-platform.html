Idea

Personal Trading Analysis Platform

One place to pull together market prices, economic data and company financials, and analyze them myself.

Why

Learning, put into practice

I'm currently pursuing the CFA. This project is how I plan to turn what I'm learning into practice: working with real data instead of only textbook examples.

The goal is a personal platform that brings together the data I care about: global markets, the US economy, commodities, currencies, crypto and company fundamentals. With it, I can test ideas, spot connections, and build my own view of what's happening.

Roadmap

Built in phases

US macro dashboard

Inflation, jobs, growth, interest rates and sentiment, from official government sources.

Planned

Global markets

Major country indexes, currencies, commodities and crypto, tracked daily.

Planned

Company fundamentals

Financial statements for S&P 500 companies, ready for ratio and valuation analysis.

Global trade & shipping

Data

What I plan to track

🏛

US economy

  • Inflation: CPI, PCE
  • Jobs: payrolls, unemployment, jobless claims
  • GDP, retail sales, housing starts
  • Money supply, consumer sentiment
Sources: FRED, BLS, BEA
％

Interest rates & credit

  • Treasury yield curve
  • Fed funds & global policy rates
  • Corporate bond spreads
  • Volatility: VIX, MOVE
Sources: FRED, US Treasury
🌐

Global stock indexes

  • US, China, Japan, Hong Kong, India
  • UK, France, Germany, Canada, Taiwan
Sources: Stooq, Yahoo Finance
📊

S&P 500 fundamentals

  • Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow
  • Margins, growth and valuation ratios
Sources: SEC EDGAR
⛏

Commodities

  • Energy: crude oil, natural gas
  • Precious metals: gold, silver, platinum
  • Industrial metals: copper, aluminum, nickel
  • Agriculture: grains, softs, livestock
  • Trader positioning (COT report)
Sources: EIA, World Bank, CFTC
🚢

Shipping & trade

  • Port calls and trade volumes
  • Container freight rates
  • Country-to-country trade flows
Sources: IMF PortWatch, Drewry, UN Comtrade
💱

Currencies

  • US dollar index
  • Major pairs: EUR, JPY, GBP
  • Emerging markets: INR, CNY, BRL
Sources: FRED, ECB
₿

Crypto

  • Bitcoin, Ethereum and major coins
  • Market cap and trading volume
Sources: CoinGecko
