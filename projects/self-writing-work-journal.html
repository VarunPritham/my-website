Idea

Self-Writing Work Journal

A private work journal that fills itself in from my emails, chats and meetings, and every morning tells me exactly where I left off.

Why

Sticky notes don't remember promises

Work is scattered across email threads, chats and meetings. A question I promised to answer at 4pm, a review someone is waiting on, a follow-up I meant to send: sticky notes and to-do lists only catch what I remember to write down.

Every morning starts with reconstructing yesterday. This idea removes that step: a journal that writes itself as I work, sorts what's done from what's still open, and briefs me the next morning so I can pick things up exactly where I left them.

How it works

Fully automatic, start to finish

Work as usualNo new app, no extra typing.
CapturedEmails, chats, meetings and calendar, picked up automatically each evening.
Sorted by AIDone, still open, promised, or waiting on others.
Written downA page per day in my private journal, searchable later.
Morning pickupA short briefing: here's where you left off.

What it tracks

Four lists, kept for me

✅

Done

  • Replies sent, work shipped, threads closed
  • A record for status updates and reviews
🔄

Still open

  • Things I started but didn't finish
🤝

I promised

  • "I'll send it tomorrow", "let me check and get back"
  • Commitments spotted in emails, chats and meeting notes
⏳

Waiting on others

  • Questions I asked that nobody has answered yet
Privacy & compliance

Stays inside the company

🏢

Company tools only

  • Runs entirely within the company's own Microsoft 365. Nothing is copied to personal apps or outside services
🔒

Private to me

  • The journal lives in my own workspace, visible only to me
💬

Only my conversations

  • Uses only the emails, chats and meetings I'm already part of
📋

Policy-friendly

  • Built on approved tools, following the company's retention and security rules
