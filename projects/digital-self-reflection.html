Idea

Digital Self-Reflection App

A quiet mirror for where my attention actually goes, across the apps I choose on my iPhone and laptop.

Why

Life is limited. So is attention.

Our time is finite, and so is the mind's attention. Left alone, it drifts toward whatever the feed serves next, not where we would choose to point it. Pulling it back to where it should be starts with seeing clearly, and that takes self-reflection.

Nearly 30% of each day is now spent on screens. That's a large share of a life, and worth looking at honestly. This app is a mirror for that time: it notices what I actually consume in the apps I choose, so I can look back and decide whether that's where I want my mind to be.

It doesn't block or nag, and it isn't another screen-time counter. It's honest data for reflection.

How it works

One tap, then it stays out of the way

Open a chosen appA prompt appears when I open any app I've marked to reflect on.
One tapI start the session. It covers every chosen app and ends on its own after 10 minutes away.
Read on the phoneText is read on the device and images are deleted instantly.
Analyzed overnightUploaded on Wi-Fi in the background and analyzed nightly, or whenever I ask.
ReflectA private dashboard on my phone and the web.
What it shows

Four ways to look back

🧭

What I consume

  • Topics across apps
  • World news, finance, movies, books, learning, entertainment…
  • How the mix shifts over weeks and months
🌤

How it affects me

  • A quick mood check after sessions
  • Shown next to what I was consuming
🎯

Chosen vs. pushed

  • What I went looking for
  • What the algorithm or a notification pulled me into
✨

Quality & novelty

  • How varied my sources are
  • How often I see the same story retold, and how often I learn something new
Privacy by design

Personal data, treated that way

🖼

No images kept

  • Screens are read on the phone, then deleted immediately
💬

Chats stay private

  • Messages and DMs are never read
🔒

Encrypted, then deleted

  • Raw text is encrypted in the cloud and deleted within 30 days
  • Only the results are kept
👤

For my eyes only

  • Behind my own login, never shared
