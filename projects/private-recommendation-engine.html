Idea

Private Recommendation Engine

Recommendations built from who you really are, learned from people with similar tastes, with every piece of data encrypted, even while it's being matched.

Why

Better choices, without giving up privacy

Most recommendations today come from platforms that know a lot about us, keep it, and optimize for their own goals, not ours. They see a thin slice of who we are: one app's clicks, one service's history.

Yet there are 8 billion people in the world. For almost any choice (what to do this weekend, where to eat in a new city, which activity to pick up next), someone with very similar tastes has already tried the options and knows what was worth it.

The idea: represent each person richly, as a set of vectors kept on their own device, and learn from people with similar vectors, without anyone's data ever being exposed. Not even to the system doing the matching.

How it works

From personal profile to shared wisdom

Build my profileMy tastes, activities and personality, as private vectors on my phone.
Log experiencesWhat I did, and how it went.
What works for mePatterns from my own history.
People with similar tastesWhat they loved, matched anonymously and encrypted.
Shared plansLater: join activities with like-minded people.

Most of this comes from the "download your data" options services already offer. Everything is processed on my own devices, and only encrypted vectors ever leave. Private messages are never used.

Privacy first

Encrypted at all times, even while matching

📱

Lives on the device

  • The full profile and history never leave the phone
🔐

Encrypted before it leaves

  • Only a compressed, encrypted summary is shared
🧮

Matched without being seen

  • Similarity is computed inside secure hardware, and later directly on encrypted data (homomorphic encryption), so no person or server sees anyone's vectors
👥

Truly anonymous

  • No names, rotating IDs
  • Results appear only when many similar people stand behind them
